# Stickers, decks and holidays

Part of The Deck brief. Rules in `CLAUDE.md` apply here too.

## Weekly decks and stickers (Phase 1.5)

The sticker loop is pulled forward from Phase 3. Each kid earns one sticker per finished routine and places it on **this week's skateboard deck**.

- **The loop:** each routine has an "Earns a sticker" switch, with at most 4 sticker routines a day. The Point shows Today's stickers (one slot per routine, a "?" mystery slot for the next one). My week shows the deck plus Mon–Sun columns with a slot per scheduled routine. My old decks is a wall rack of past weeks. No streaks, no missed marks, no sibling comparison.
- **Sticker mix:** stickers come from both the original collection and Fluent Emoji, at slightly varied sizes and tilts, so a deck reads as a kid's board rather than a matched set. Every sticker gets the same die-cut: a paper rim, a thin ink line and a hard offset shadow, applied the same way regardless of source (the original stickers' built-in cut is replaced by it). A thinner cut is used at the 44 px My week size.
- **Sticker variety:** no sticker repeats within a week's deck; unseen stickers are weighted up; each offer mixes both kinds; rider stickers are rare (about one offer in ten); new sets join the pool over time, and seasonal sets are only offered in season.
- **Deck designs:** 36 designs in 6 worlds × 6 looks. Worlds: Skate, Surf, Snow (board sports), Trail (mountains and woods), Tropical and Winter (snowy scenes). Looks: stripes, repeat pattern, scene, big graphic, radial, hand-drawn. Each design has 2 colorways and a light and a dark version; the deck follows day and night like the rest of the screen, and the kid's color tints every version. The full set is on the canvas (Stickers & decks page, "Deck designs — 36").
- **Weekly rotation:** a new random deck every Monday, with 3 rerolls a week that keep the stickers; a reroll always jumps to a different world. The world never repeats two weeks running, and paired worlds (Snow and Winter, Surf and Tropical) are never back to back. No design repeats within 12 weeks, and colorway 2 is held back until a design has shown once in colorway 1. The week's world lightly nudges the stickers: one of each three offered leans toward that world's set.
- **Seasonal lean:** Winter is more likely from December to February and Tropical from June to August, without being locked to those months. Holiday decks replace the rotation during their windows (see Holidays, below).
- **Holidays:** each of the nine holidays has an edge trim, a corner piece and a holiday deck in light and dark (canvas, Holidays page; applied on The Point page, Halloween and Christmas frames). The decks are Halloween: Pumpkin Patch, Thanksgiving: Harvest Stripes, Christmas: Holly Lights, New Year: Midnight Confetti, Valentine's Day: Heart Stack, St. Patrick's Day: Lucky Clover, Easter: Egg Hunt, Fourth of July: Firework Night and Birthday: Party Bunting (tinted in the kid's color).
  - **The accents:** accents overlay; they never push content down. Every kid browsing screen keeps a permanent sky band at the top (about 70 px). The edge trim hangs in it, at most 72 px tall, on a layer above the background and below all content, and it cannot be tapped. It runs from the left edge of the screen to behind the corner circle, which replaces the seasonal sun at exactly the sun's size and position, so the avatar keeps sitting on its edge. Trims are drawn full-width with their pieces repeating along the string at natural size (never stretched), the centerpiece (the North Star, the cross) at the centre and never repeated; St. Patrick's rainbow appears once, at the start of the string; winter's icicles and snowflakes keep their length patterns edge to edge; portrait and landscape each get their own width. Nothing below the sky band moves in a holiday window. There is no separate corner sticker; the holiday sticker shows on the deck, the Tour Dates countdown tile and the "?" slot in Today's stickers.
  - **Trims and circles** (canvas, Holidays page: "Holiday trims v2" for the designs and motion, "Holiday trims v3" for the full-width drawings at 640 px portrait and 1000 px landscape in day and night, and "Holiday kit"; Halloween and Christmas full-width are on The Point frames): Halloween: skulls and mini pumpkins on strings with bats flying between; a giant pumpkin with two bats. Thanksgiving: maple leaves on twine with acorns; a harvest sun with falling leaves. Christmas: a pine garland with ornaments and the North Star at the centre; a red glass ornament. New Year: curly streamers, party horns and gold stars; lilac with confetti. Valentine's Day: hearts at different lengths and two love letters; pink with paper hearts. St. Patrick's Day: a rainbow, clovers and gold coins; a gold coin stamped with a clover. Easter: the cross with sunrise rays at the centre, lilies and painted eggs; a sunrise with the cross on it. Fourth of July: stars on a string with fireworks below; navy with stars and red stripes. Birthday: pennants in the kid's color with balloons; gold with three lit candles. Winter: icicles and snowflakes on strings, hung from the winter circle on non-holiday days from New Year through February.
  - **When:** accents show from 14 days before the Tour Dates entry through the day itself, the same window as the holiday stickers. The holiday deck replaces the weekly rotation for the Monday-to-Sunday week the holiday falls in; Christmas gets the two weeks before December 25. Rerolls are paused that week; stickers carry over as usual.
  - **Where:** only on browsing screens (The Point, My week, Tour Dates). Never on task screens (routines, Wave Check, breathing, Session), never on celebrations, and never in focus volume.
  - **Motion:** each trim has its own entrance, stepped, about 1.2 s, once a day on the first visit to The Point: bats flutter in, leaves fall and sway, ornaments drop and the North Star pops, streamers unfurl, hearts drop like a mobile, coins flip, eggs drop as lilies and the cross pop, stars drop as fireworks pop, balloons float up, snowflakes drift down turning. No idle motion. Reduce Motion: they simply appear.
  - **Parent controls:** Back Office has one master "Holiday accents" switch, plus a switch per holiday that turns its trim, deck and stickers on or off together.
  - **Birthdays:** only the birthday kid gets the trim and the Party Bunting deck, in their birthday week; the sibling sees just a cake on the countdown tile. A birthday beats a holiday on that kid's screens; if two holiday windows overlap, the nearer date wins.
- **Sticker placement:** each new sticker lands in a random but balanced spot. The app samples about two dozen points inside a safe zone (inset from the nose, tail, top and bottom edges), scores each by its distance from the stickers already on the deck plus a bonus for the half with fewer stickers, and picks at random from the top three. Size varies from 86 to 96 px and tilt from ±5° to ±12° (no tilt in focus volume). The spot, size and tilt are saved with the sticker, so the deck looks the same on every screen and every visit (The Point, My week, My old decks).
- **Seasonal drops:** worlds can grow. Starting in year two, 2–3 new Tropical decks are added before summer and 2–3 new Winter decks before December; a new design gets first-week priority in its season. Adding a design is a data change (pattern plus colors), not an app change.

## My stickers (Phase 1.5)

- Sticker Wall moves inside My week as **My stickers**: My week's header gets "My stickers" and "Old decks" buttons, and the dock keeps lighting My week.
- Every sticker the kid has, filtered by set (All, Sea, Dinos, Space, Boards, Holidays, School), with an ×2 badge for doubles and a NEW tag; tap one for a big view with "Got it Thu, Oct 1" and Hear it. Only stickers the kid has are shown: no locked silhouettes or empty slots, which would read as missed.
- Pre-reader: icon-only set buttons, five bigger stickers per row, no names (tap speaks the name). Focus volume: flat ground, no tilts or offset headline. The shared jar (a Sticker Wall goal both kids fill, Phase 3) will live here too.
